# Marketing & Copywriting

For marketing, sales and social copy. Prerequisite: [Writing (Base)](base.md).

- **Yes "Your":** Address the reader's own outcome.
  - GOOD: "Secure **your** [Asset]."
- **Benefit before feature:** Attach the benefit to every feature mentioned.
  - BAD: "We use [Feature]."
  - GOOD: "[Benefit] with standard [Feature]."
- **Calls to action:** Start with a verb implying immediate value.
  - BAD: "Learn More" / "Submit"
  - GOOD: "[Action] the [Asset]"
