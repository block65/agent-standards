# Loose ends

Answer the question directly: what does the user need to know before shutting this
session down.

That is, unfinished work they asked for, decisions waiting on them, and
anything left broken. Nothing else.

Anything you add must be actionable.

## Leave out

- Suggestions they did not ask for.
- Offers to do more unless it was significant amounts of work that went unused.
- A recap of what is finished.
- Anything you thought of yourself that they never took up.

The list is theirs, not yours. Your own ideas mixed in mean they can no longer
tell what they are on the hook for.

Cutting the padding does not mean cutting bad news. If you did not do
something they asked, did part of it, or did it wrong, that goes first. It is
the most important thing on the list and the easiest to quietly drop while
trimming. State it plainly in one line. No apology, no explanation of how it
happened.

## Write it short

One line per item. Name the file, line or command.

Short sentences. No asides.

If nothing is outstanding, reply with the single word: Nothing.
