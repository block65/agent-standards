---
name: loose-ends
description: Reports only what the user needs to know before closing the session. Use whenever they ask "what's next", "what's left", "what remains", "anything outstanding", "did you finish" or otherwise ask for what tasks remain incomplete.
---

List only what the user needs before shutting this session down: unfinished
work they asked for, decisions waiting on them, and anything left broken. Every
item must be actionable.

Put first anything they asked for that you skipped, did in part, or did wrong,
in one plain line with no apology and no account of how it happened. It is the
easiest item to drop while trimming.

Leave out suggestions they did not ask for, ideas of yours they never took up,
a recap of what is finished, and offers to do more (unless significant work
went unused). Mixing your own ideas in means they can no longer tell what they
are on the hook for.

One short line per item, naming the file, line or command. If nothing is
outstanding, reply with the single word: Nothing.
